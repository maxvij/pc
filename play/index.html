    How it works

    Every game has eleven traits, each scored 0 to 3: story, reflexes, thinking, open world, challenge, chill, building, realism, graphics, length and short sessions. They're worked out from the tags Steam players vote onto each game and from median playtime, then calibrated against about 190 games tagged by hand. Review labels follow Steam's own thresholds; Metacritic scores come from the Steam store.

    Your settings become weights. A game scores higher the more its traits agree with them, the more it resembles the games you loved, and the better it's rated on Steam. The list then spreads across genres so one kind of game doesn't take over.

    Nothing you enter leaves your browser. Prices come live from CheapShark.
