Getting back into PC · October 2026

What changed since your 3080 build.

Short explainers, what each means for your silver T1, and the best things to read or watch.

The big shifts since 2022

  • DDR5 and AM5 are the default. DDR4 platforms are done; AMD's AM5 socket runs through at least 2027 and takes Zen 6.
  • X3D chips own gaming. AMD's stacked-cache CPUs (7800X3D, 9800X3D) beat everything else in games while using little power.
  • Upscaling got much better. DLSS 4's transformer model cut ghosting; DLAA gives the same tech at native resolution, no upscaling.
  • Ray tracing went mainstream, and path tracing arrived. It's the main reason GPUs still struggle at native resolution.
  • OLED monitors became normal for gaming at 240–500 Hz.
  • 2026 is a memory crunch. AI demand pushed DDR5, GDDR7 and SSD prices up sharply, which is why used deals matter now.

Explainers

DDR5, EXPO and memory training

DDR5 boots at a default 4800 MT/s. Turning on EXPO in the BIOS runs it at its rated speed. On AM5, 6000 MT/s CL30 is the sweet spot.

The first boot after building or enabling EXPO can show a black screen for a minute or more while the board "trains" the memory. That's normal: don't power off.

For you: enable EXPO, then turn on Memory Context Restore so later boots skip full training.
EXPO explained · XDA: the BIOS setting that halves AM5 boot time

PCIe 5.0

Double the bandwidth of PCIe 4.0, but games barely use it. Tests show a 5090 or 5080 on PCIe 4.0 loses only about 1–3%.

For you: the T1's PCIe 5.0 riser is future-proof, but a 40-series card on PCIe 4.0 loses nothing meaningful.
TechPowerUp PCIe scaling · Gamers Nexus PCIe scaling

Mini-ITX

A 17 × 17 cm board: one GPU slot, two RAM slots, usually two M.2 slots, fewer fan headers. Modern B850 ITX boards lose little else.

For you: buy 2 sticks now at the size you want long-term. There's no adding a third and fourth later.
Newegg form-factor guide 2026

Gen4 vs Gen5 SSDs

Gen5 drives are faster on paper but run hot, can throttle in small cases, and don't load games noticeably faster. DirectStorage support is still rare.

For you: a good Gen4 2 TB drive is the right pick for a 10-litre case.
XDA: Gen5 throttling · Gen4 vs Gen5 for gaming

The 12V-2×6 GPU connector

The new 16-pin GPU power plug. Melting cases almost always trace to a plug that isn't fully seated.

For you: push until it clicks, check there's no visible gap on both ends, and don't bend the cable sharply right at the plug. Use the SF850's native cable, no adapters.
Tom's Hardware: melting connectors and how to avoid them

X3D chips

AMD stacks extra cache on the CPU, which games love. They run at modest power, and the 7800X3D has a lower 89 °C limit.

For you: a light negative Curve Optimizer (−10 to −20) lowers temps for free. Test stability after each step.
TechPowerUp 7800X3D tuning · 9800X3D Curve Optimizer guide

Quiet and cool, step by step

  1. Frame cap in the NVIDIA app or RTSS, at 90–120 fps. The single biggest noise cut.
  2. GPU undervolt in MSI Afterburner (free software that works with any NVIDIA card). Start around 900 mV at stock clocks: typically 30–60 W less, 5–10 °C cooler, same fps. 5080 undervolt guide
  3. CPU Curve Optimizer at −10 to −20 in the BIOS.
  4. Fan curves with the free FanControl app: tie the two Noctua top fans to the hotter of CPU and GPU. XDA FanControl guide
  5. DLAA instead of TAA in games that offer it: native resolution, cleaner edges, no upscaling.
Your Noctua G2 fans already include an anti-vibration gasket (NA-AVG1), the rubber layer Optimum recommends between fan and frame.

Your T1, specifically

  • RAM height: standard-height kits (~35 mm, like the PNY XLR8 or Corsair Vengeance) are fine. Avoid tall Dominator or Trident Z kits.
  • Before the first boot: update the BIOS with the flashback button, seat the 12V-2×6 until it clicks, and expect a long first memory-training boot.
