    Recommendations

    • GPU. 5080 for RT Ultra at 60+. 5070 Ti if RT High is fine and the NZ$700 matters. A used 4080 Super only at a clear discount. Skip the 5080 Super unless 24 GB matters more than heat.
    • CPU. 7800X3D new at ≈ NZ$609; the 9800X3D isn't worth NZ$250 more. Stay on AM5 for the Zen 6 upgrade path unless Nova Lake reviews are exceptional.
    • Cool and quiet. Cap the frame rate, undervolt or power-limit the GPU, air cooler plus the two Noctua top exhausts.
